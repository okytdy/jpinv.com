01 · Share-price regime

What moved the share price over the past two years?

Rakumachi is a site that connects real estate companies across the country who want to sell profitable properties such as rental apartments, parking lots, and small commercial buildings with individual investors who want to buy them. Real estate companies pay a monthly fee to list properties. It has been ranked high in searches for "income real estate" for many years, and its YouTube channel that explains real estate investment has surpassed 1 billion views by the end of 2025, so it costs almost nothing to attract users.

01 · The rally Rakumachi’s main product is its income-property marketplace. In January 2026, about 487,000 individual investors used it free while 5,459 agencies paid monthly listing fees. Three smaller revenue streams add a patented matching feature, Rakumachi Premium subscriptions with H1 FY7/26 ARPU up 17%, and sponsored videos for property managers and financial institutions. Margins benefit from low acquisition costs: twenty years of search visibility and a YouTube channel exceeding one billion cumulative views by end-2025 draw users, while Premium and sponsored videos have pricing scope. Separately, ¥4.08bn of US-dollar corporate bonds held to maturity yield an average 5.5%, producing roughly ¥0.114bn interest every six months below OP. These funds earn about one-fifth of the core business’s return and depress group returns unless released for uses such as buybacks. The shares nearly tripled from ¥501 on May 16, 2024 to ¥1,479 on September 16, 2025. FY7/24 results in September 2024 showed sales up 13% to ¥2.36bn and a 44.9% margin, alongside about ¥4bn allocated to bonds (nine securities acquired for ¥4.0089bn). September 12, 2025 FY7/25 results showed sales up 33.6% to ¥3.16bn and OP up 45.6% to ¥1.55bn, with margin at 48.9%. The same day brought the CEO transition from founder Naohiro Sakaguchi to long-serving marketing leader Ryo Fujie and the fourth buyback in twelve months, capped at 500,000 shares and ¥0.75bn.

02 · The reversal The shares lost about one-third of their gains in eight months after September’s high, reaching ¥990 on March 13, 2026 despite strong results. October 2025’s AGM expanded the board from four to seven, adding two long-serving department heads as internal directors. The founder’s 68.2% stake remained unchanged, but day-to-day oversight broadened. December’s Q1 FY7/26 results showed OP up 80.4% and sales up 17.0%; March’s H1 showed OP up 43.4%, sales up 14.9%, a 55.3% margin and ¥1.757bn revenue. The decline did not reflect business slowing. September’s near-tripling from the May 2024 ¥501 low was more plausibly formed in thin trading. The Sakaguchi family owns 68.2%; VIS Advisors LP at 5.08% is the only other disclosed holder above 5%. Institutions can trade only about one-quarter of outstanding shares. Limited float amplifies small purchases and can equally amplify declines when buying stops.

03 · Current position On May 25, 2026 management announced a buyback of up to 600,000 shares, 3.09% excluding treasury shares, for ¥0.5bn through November 27, alongside corrections to page views in six releases since September 2025’s FY7/25 results. Only PV changed; revenue and profit did not, because agencies pay by listing count rather than views. Overstatement increased in newer releases: FY7/25 PV fell 0.6%, Q1 FY7/26 5.6%, and H1 8.5%, reducing H1 YoY growth from 23.2% to 12.7%. Management only said that a review of internal aggregation found errors. Watch whether it publishes the revised method and several years of monthly PV so membership growth can be reconciled to data; whether the policy for ¥4.08bn of bonds changes, as they represent about two-thirds of assets but earn only one-fifth of the core business’s return; and whether the income-property marketplace’s competitive strength is still increasing or growth is maturing. Forward EV/EBIT is 11.3x, above domestic digital marketplace peers’ median of 5.7x.
