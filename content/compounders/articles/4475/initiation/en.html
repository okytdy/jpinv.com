01 · Stock price trends

What moved the share price over the past two years?

The stock price was around 1,000 yen in May 2024, and rose to 1,899 yen in August 2025. However, over the next six months, most of the gains were lost. The trigger was not a deterioration in business performance, but the next year's plan announced in November 2025 and the slowdown in ARR growth over the following two quarters.

01 · The rally In spring 2024, HENNGE was valued as a solid domestic identity-management and email-security SaaS business, with limited grounds for a large growth premium. Strong FY24 and FY25 quarterly results changed that view. FY24 sales rose 25% and OP 71%; during FY25, HENNGE One ARR entered the ¥10bn range and Basic-to-Pro migration appeared to be progressing. About 3,500 customers, a gross margin rising from 84.1% in FY24 to 86.5% in FY25, and monthly gross revenue churn falling to 0.33% suggested low churn and room to upsell. The shares reached ¥1,899 on August 14, 2025.

02 · The reversal The outlook changed in November 2025. FY25 sales grew 30.6%, OP 76.7%, and ARR reached ¥11.1bn. However, FY26 guidance combined 17.5% sales growth with a 16.0% OP margin, little changed from FY25’s 16.4%. Management was investing in endpoint and managed security, domain protection and a US joint venture. These were longer-term investments, but short-term investors saw slower growth and higher costs. ARR growth slowed from 20.6% in February 2026 Q1 to 14.7% in May Q2. Q2 also showed fewer users per customer as new business skewed toward smaller companies and some large existing customers chose single-feature plans instead of Pro. The market discounted the Pro-driven ARPU growth story. The price reached ¥973 on March 13, 2026, nearly halving from August’s high.

03 · Current position Management bought back shares during the decline. On November 21, 2025 it authorized up to 700,000 shares and ¥1.19bn; by December 19 it had acquired all 700,000 for ¥0.909988bn, averaging about ¥1,300, with completion disclosed on December 22. The purpose was to prepare for share compensation and stock-funded M&A, control dilution and improve capital efficiency. This did not reverse the valuation decline. The May 12, 2026 close of ¥1,016 was above March’s low but well below August’s high. Business quality remained strong: ROCE about 48%, gross margin 86.8% and FCF yield about 8%. Next-twelve-month EV/EBIT was roughly 12.5x, versus around 22x at the high. The next valuation depends on explanations of ARR growth, Pro migration, upfront investment and capital allocation.

02 · Issue

Three questions dividing investor views

There are three points that

investors are trying to determine. Will ARR growth accelerate again? Will the company be able to maintain a sufficient profit margin after investing in growth? Will it continue to buy back its own shares with discipline?

Issue 01 · Slowdown in ARR growth rate
Is the ARR growth rate of +14.7% a temporary slowdown, or will the future growth rate remain at this level?
bullish The bullish view is that although the ARR growth rate alone has slowed, the fundamental KPIs have not collapsed yet. bearish The bearish view is that the quality of growth has changed. New acquisitions are focused on mid-sized and small businesses, and the number of users per contract is decreasing. Some of the existing major companies are choosing single functions instead of Pro, and advertising expenses will increase by 20.1% in FY26 plan, exceeding the sales plan of +17.5%. Q4 ARR growth rate in FY26+12% or lessIf it falls to this level, it will be interpreted as a structural slowdown, not just a baseline effect.
Issue 02 · Suppression of profit margin
Is the operating profit margin plan of 16.0% for FY26 intended to advance growth investment, or does it indicate a limit to profitability?
bullish The bullish view is that the earning power itself is high. The gross profit margin is86.8%, capital investment is sales0.7%, almost no interest-bearing debt, and ROCE isApproximately 48%becomes. H1's operating profit margin is20.7%The difference from the full-year plan of 16.0% can be interpreted as being due to the inclusion of branding, US JV, and new product launch costs first. FY26 results17% or moreIf you land on this, it will be easier to evaluate the company as one that can maintain profit discipline while making investments.
bearish The bearish view is that external factors will put pressure on profit margins. Microsoft E5 includes Defender for Office 365 and Entra ID, which overlaps with HENNGE One's Email DLP/ID area. Additionally, a high proportion of AWS-related expenses are denominated in dollars, and a weaker yen will reduce gross profit margins. FY26 operating profit marginless than 14%If the forecast is revised downward, the market may perceive it as a ceiling on profitability rather than an advance in investment.
Issue 03 · Capital allocation
Was ¥0.91bn of share buyback a step toward improving capital efficiency, or was it a purchase at a high price?
bullish The bullish view appreciates that the company has started using its balance sheet. Stock buybacks from November to December 2025¥0.90998bn・700,000 sharesThis is equivalent to 2.2% of the number of outstanding shares after deducting treasury stock. The message is that it is not just about accumulating cash on hand, as it also has the meaning of reducing dilution from stock compensation. If FY26 full-year results indicate additional share buybacks or a continued return policy, confidence in capital allocation will likely increase.
bearish A bearish view focuses on the level at which the stock was purchased and the ambiguity of the explanation. Acquisition will be from November 26, 2025 to December 19, 2025, with an average of approx.1,300 yenIt was held in At that time, the actual ARR growth rate was approximately 31 times, and had already slowed down. Why would a company that can reinvest in a high ROCE business buy its own stock at that price? If this point is not explained, it will remain a question of the discipline of capital allocation rather than shareholder returns. If no additional policies are indicated in the FY26 full-year financial results and only cash accumulates, this issue will be brought to mind again.
