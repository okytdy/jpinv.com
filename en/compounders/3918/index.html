Section 03 · Earnings Trend · By Segment

Where the profit comes from

Segment figures from the tanshin segment notes (JP GAAP, ¥M, including inter-segment sales). The three-segment structure (Engineering / Products & Devices / ICT Solutions) was introduced with FY9/2024, and the fiscal calendar changed in 2025, so only the transitional 6-month period and FY3/2026 are shown on a comparable basis. Segment profits exceed group operating profit because goodwill amortization (FY3/2026: ¥181M) and holding-company costs sit in an adjustment line (FY3/2026: -¥730M). Per-quarter segment splits are disclosed in each tanshin. From Q2 FY3/2027, the semiconductor design/test subsidiary Privatech (50%-owned) leaves the Products & Devices and ICT Solutions segments as it deconsolidates to the equity method.

2026-06-02 JII Compounder Profile — Initiation ↗

PCI Holdings is an embedded-software and IoT engineering group, 51% owned by trading company Restar. After doubling off its 2025 low to ¥1,607 on the PCI-VISION2027 plan, the stock gave back a quarter as guidance landed below the plan's own 9.0% operating-margin target; it trades near book with net cash worth about a third of market value. The debate is the margin bridge, an unused buyback, and how a 51% parent treats minority holders.
