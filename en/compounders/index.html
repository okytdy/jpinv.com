Nippon Gear 6356TSE Standard

32.5% service margins, 4.7× forward EV/EBIT

Valve actuators installed in power stations and water systems can remain in service for 30–50 years. Nippon Gear handles the inspections and repairs throughout that life.

The investment case

Net cash accounts for ¥475 of the ¥1,299 share price. The remaining operating business is valued at 4.69× guided operating profit. The two open questions are whether product margins hold and how management uses ¥6.77 billion of net cash.
