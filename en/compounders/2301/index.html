2026-06-08 JII Compounder Profile — Initiation ↗

Gakujo runs Japan's leading recruiting media and job fairs for people in their 20s — the Re-Shukatsu career-change site with 2.8m members, an agent business, and new-graduate services. On June 8, 2026 it cut FY2026 operating-profit guidance to ¥2.6bn and authorized a ¥650M buyback; with ¥11.3bn of cash and securities — over half its market value — the debt-free, founder-controlled operating business trades near 5.5x EV/EBIT.
