BASIC POLICY · 骨太の方針

Read 25 years of Japan’s budget blueprint.

The “honebuto” (Basic Policy on Economic and Fiscal Management and Reform) is the government’s annual policy framework, Cabinet-decided each June and serving as the blueprint for the next year’s budget. From Koizumi in 2001 to Takaichi in 2026, this tool maps — for foreign institutional investors — what each administration promised, how well it delivered, and where 2026 is heading.
