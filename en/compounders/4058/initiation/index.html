01 · REGIME

What moved the share price over the past two years?

Toyokumo is a SaaS company that develops multiple cloud software for small and medium-sized enterprises. The stock price over the past two years reflects how the market has reevaluated the company's three businesses: safety confirmation, kintone collaboration, and NotePM. It was bought from ¥1,287 in August 2024 to ¥3,780 in August 2025, and after that, there was a major adjustment until spring 2026, and some of it was revised in the first quarter results on May 14th. Rather than just looking at stock prices, it is more accurate to read along with how business is going.

01 · The rally Toyokumo was founded in 2010 as a Cybozu subsidiary, became independent through founder Yuji Yamamoto’s 2014 MBO, and listed on Mothers, now Growth, in 2020. Anpi Confirmation Service 2 provides employee safety confirmation for disaster response. Its second pillar, kintone integration, includes FormBridge, kViewer and kMailer, extending Cybozu’s no-code platform into external forms, public views, email and automated workflows. Cybozu remains the second-largest shareholder with 7.30%, or 800,000 shares, so the relationship also has an equity foundation. November 2023’s Toyokumo Cloud Connect, a majority-owned joint venture with Cybozu as minority shareholder, deepened collaboration and signaled preparation for further M&A. Free trials converting to paid use helped contracts reach 20,220 in Q1 FY26. Cross-selling across seven products raised ARPA to ¥21,329, up 9.9% YoY, while the kintone customer base also provided a channel for acquired products. The January 2025 Project Mode acquisition added NotePM knowledge-management SaaS as a third pillar. The shares rose from ¥1,287 in August 2024, nearly doubled by spring 2025 as FY24 earnings were reassessed, then reached ¥3,780 on August 19. About 22x forward EV/EBIT implied that the distribution platform built over ten years, with strong recognition as a third-party integration provider and more than 15,000 paid contracts at its peak, would benefit NotePM and TCC immediately.

02 · The reversal The reversal began in autumn as FY25 results became clearer. On November 13, 2025 management raised sales guidance by about ¥200M and OP by ¥100M, a modest upgrade relative to expectations embedded at the August high. Investors saw integration benefits being absorbed gradually rather than a decisive margin step-up. FY25 results on February 13, 2026 showed consolidated sales of ¥4,858M (+54.4%) and OP of ¥1,605M. Project Mode consolidation added 16ppt to growth; parent-only sales growth of 38.8% better indicated the existing business’s momentum. The roughly 33% OP margin combined a subscription gross margin near 95%, annual Project Mode goodwill amortization of ¥146M through FY31, and discretionary advertising. Advertising had the greatest effect on the shares: FY25 spending was ¥1,278M, including ¥1,248M at Toyokumo itself for television and web advertising. This supported kintone-related customer access and brand recognition rather than NotePM search advertising. FY26 guidance of ¥5,800M sales and ¥1,900M OP assumed additional advertising pressure on margins. The same day, management authorized its third buyback, ¥300M and 150,000 shares, the largest by value after ¥200M and 100,000 shares in February 2025 and a smaller September 2022 program. January and February monthly releases appeared to indicate slowing growth, and the price fell to ¥1,943 by mid-May, about 49% below August’s high.

03 · Current position Two releases shortly before publication could change the assessment. April 2026 monthly sales growth of 33.2% YoY suggested reacceleration rather than the slowdown inferred earlier in the year. May 14 Q1 FY26 results showed sales of ¥1,389M (+29.0%), OP of ¥600M (+80.5%) and net profit of ¥401M (+82.9%). The 43.2% OP margin was about 10ppt above full-year guidance of 32.8%. Advertising fell 14.5% YoY to ¥248M, showing restraint in Q1 rather than the increase implied by guidance. Parent-only recurring metrics were ARR of ¥5,738M (+24.5%), churn of 0.81%, 20,220 paid contracts (+13.3%), ARPA of ¥21,329 (+9.9%) and a company-defined Rule of 40 score of 72.2. Guidance remained unchanged, and advertising’s usual H2 concentration may simply have made Q1 the quietest quarter. By April’s end, the February buyback was 66% complete by value: ¥197.8M of ¥300M and 108,700 of 150,000 shares. Over the next four quarters, watch whether advertising discipline is structural or reflects timing, how much NotePM contributes to consolidated profit, and whether management formalizes a policy for ¥4.4bn of cash against working capital needs of about ¥0.5bn, or keeps it as discretionary M&A funding.

Issue 02 · Return on investment of NotePM
Does the acquisition of Project Mode generate profits that exceed the amortization of goodwill?
bullish The bullish side is due to Project Mode acquisition (acquisition price ¥1,330M, goodwill recorded amount ¥1,028M, 7-year amortization)Goodwill amortization of ¥146M in 2018Even with this burden, the consolidated operating profit margin will be lower in FY2533%, in FY26 plan32.8%Look at what maintains this. TCC/PM sales including NotePM increased in each quarter of FY25 (¥117M→¥149M).NotePM ARR and TCC breakdown, or the operating profit margin by product in the FY26 interim financial results, it will be easier to confirm whether the acquisition is generating profits on a customer-by-customer basis.
bearish The bears see insufficient disclosure. Toyokumo reports its performance in a single segment, and even after the acquisition, it does not sufficiently show the profit margin by product or the number of subscribers for NotePM alone, making it difficult for investors to verify whether the company is recovering its acquisition funds. NotePM is an information sharing SaaS for domestic small and medium-sized enterprises that competes with Notion, esa, DocBase, etc. It is also possible that much of the advertising costs in FY25 were used not to acquire customers for NotePM, but to maintain the popularity of kintone's peripheral products. The high consolidated operating profit margin can be explained by the fact that existing businesses support the entire group. The acquisition itself may have little impact on profits on a customer-by-customer basis, or may even slightly lower profit margins. If the company maintains a consolidated profit margin of around 33% even in its FY26 financial results without disclosing information specific to NotePM, it can be interpreted that verification of investment recovery has been postponed.
