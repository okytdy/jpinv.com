01 · Share-price regime

What moved the share price over the past two years?

From the low price ¥3,115 on August 5, 2024 to the high price ¥5,820 on July 17, 2025, the stock price increased +87% in about 11 months. Behind this are disclosures showing improvement in profit margins, review of return policy, and presentation of a three-year medium-term plan. After that, the stock was adjusted by about 30% in about nine months, and the next evaluation will be determined by three points: “valuation,” “support for dividends and share buybacks,” and “monetization of MBSE.”

01 · The rally Zuken’s software turns circuit designs into working printed circuit boards, automotive wire harnesses and defense and aerospace systems. It has five product families. Alongside Japanese OEMs, its 2006 Sim-Team acquisition deepened E3.series adoption among European industrial equipment and rail manufacturers. Design data accumulated over years are hard to migrate, underpinning a stable customer base over its 50-year history. CR-8000 and E3.series license sales lead to annual maintenance fees, while DS manages design data across product generations. Client Services reached 42.9% of FY3/26 sales and grew 9.1%, versus 3.5% license growth. Backlog was ¥26.22bn (+25%), about six months’ sales. Three disclosures supported the rise from ¥3,115 on August 5, 2024 to ¥5,820 on July 17, 2025: November 2024’s DOE target of at least 5%; May 2025 FY3/25 sales growth of 5.9% and OP growth of 12.4%, with maintenance and Japan improving mix; and a ¥3bn buyback alongside a three-year plan for ¥49bn sales and ¥7.4bn OP by FY3/28.

02 · The reversal The turning point was August 6, 2025. Q1 FY3/26 sales of ¥9.119bn (+1.1%) and OP of ¥0.827bn (−3.4%) looked like a slowdown, although the medium-term plan concentrated growth in years two and three and higher personnel costs reflected upfront MBSE development. The shares fell 23% from ¥5,820 to ¥4,500 by late January 2026. Disclosures nevertheless supported a stronger H2. Artisan Partners disclosed an 8.11% stake in October 2025; November added a ¥100 50th-anniversary dividend, taking FY3/26 DPS to ¥200. December’s plan update organized the strategy around semiconductors, autonomous AI and model-based systems engineering and announced participation in Resonac’s JOINT3 consortium for next-generation organic panel packaging. Investors still treated upfront costs as execution risk, leaving the price weak despite growing backlog.

03 · Current position May 14, 2026 FY3/26 results supported the H2 recovery: sales were ¥43.101bn (+5.8%) and OP ¥5.865bn (+8.8%), 4.7% above ¥5.6bn guidance. Record equity-method income of ¥0.9bn from Business Engineering lifted ordinary profit to ¥7.134bn (+20.2%). OP margin rose 40bp to 13.6%. FY3/27 guidance envisaged 6.7% sales growth and OP growth accelerating from 8.8% to 14.2%; the ordinary dividend rose 50% from FY3/25’s ¥100 to ¥150. May 2025’s ¥3bn buyback completed in late January 2026, following near-full execution of FY3/25’s ¥2.5bn authorization. LTM total payout reached 134% of net profit and treasury shares 5.3%. Watch whether ¥6.7bn FY3/27 OP guidance is conservative against the final-year ¥7.4bn target, whether the Americas return to profit after four loss-making years since FY3/23 as GENESYS-led MBSE sales approach FY3/28’s ¥3bn target, and how ¥36.1bn of remaining cash is allocated.

02 · Investor issues

Three questions dividing investor views

Based on

's most recent financial results and the Q&A at the FY3/26 financial results briefing, we will summarize three points of contention that will affect stock valuation.

Issue 01 · Capital allocation
Is ¥36.1bn of cash supporting the flexible M&A? Or is it surplus funds that are pushing down on capital efficiency, given that the return on capital in the main business is five times the interest rate on deposits?
bullish Bulls evaluate the fact that the company has carried out share buybacks to almost 100% of the upper limit for two consecutive years (FY3/25 ¥2.5bn, FY3/26 ¥3bn). In November 2024, the company introduced a policy of increasing DOE to 5% or more, and also raised the ordinary dividend by 50% for FY3/27. The total return rate for FY3/26 reached 134%, indicating that shareholder returns are no longer a temporary measure and cash growth has begun to come to a halt.This view will hold true if the company announces its policy of continuing share buybacks of more than ¥3bn per year or raising DOE to more than 6% in stages in the FY3/27 final financial year (May 2027), and if cash and deposits and short-term securities at the end of FY3/27 fall below ¥36.1bn.
bearish Bearish groups believe that even with ¥3bn of share buyback on FY3/26, its cash balance has increased, and that the 10-year "flexible M&A" framework is It was pointed out that only one bolt-on project (¥1.6bn) was produced, and that the FY3/27 planned ROE decreased from 13.0% in FY3/26 to 11.2% as surplus visualized costs.This view holds if cash and deposits and short-term securities exceed ¥37bn at the end of FY3/27, there is no additional approval beyond the share buyback already in progress, and there is no M&A of ¥3bn or more completed within the fiscal year.
Issue 02 · MBSE business
Will GENESYS become the second growth business towards FY3/28, or will the Americas business continue to be in the red?
bullish The bullish opinion is that the US segment operating deficit in FY3/26 was halved from -785 million yen in the previous year to -392 million yen, that participation in the JOINT3 consortium led by Resonac and collaboration with IBM on chip mounting for AI means external approval of chip and package design positioning, and that FY3/28 of the medium-term plan MBSE sales target ¥3bn implies a CAGR of 15.1% compared to FY3/25 actual ¥1.97bn, which is considered to be consistent with the trend already underway.This view will hold true if the America segment achieves operating profit or break-even point in Q4 FY3/27 (May 2027), and MBSE sales at the midpoint of the medium-term plan are disclosed at ¥2.5bn or higher.
bearish Bears point out that commercial MBSE buyers are also customers of Cadence Helium, Siemens Polarion, and Mentor E3D tool chains, which are areas where Zuken does not have an installed base. The 15.1% CAGR is a claim, not evidence, and neither the number of customers nor the mix of licenses and pilots is disclosed.This view will hold true if the US segment operating deficit in FY3/27 is over ¥300 million, and MBSE sales at the midpoint of the medium-term plan are disclosed at less than ¥2bn.
Issue 03 · Profitability by region
Will the Asian business be able to maintain an operating profit margin of 29.2% even if sales exceed ¥2.14bn of scale? Will European profit margins return to over 10%? Or is Japan the only country where profits can grow stably?
bullish Bulls read the fact that the Asia segment achieved an operating profit margin of 29.2% on sales of ¥2.14bn as evidence that the software model will cross borders, and that the missing variable is scale, and that the Indian CR-8000 lamp, which is listed in the medium-term plan, is the next vector. The decline in profit margins in Europe (11.0% → 9.3%) is due to FX and product mix issues, and the installed base of E3.series on railways and power grids continues to be the de facto standard.This view will hold true if Asian sales grow by more than 12% year-on-year in FY3/27, the operating profit margin remains above 28%, and the European operating profit margin returns to above 10%.
bearish Bearish groups point out that 30 years of overseas business has produced only one structural victory in Europe and one surplus in Asia based on small sales, and that the medium-term plan only incorporates overseas sales + ¥2.8bn until FY3/28, compared to additional sales in Japan + ¥5.5bn. The friction in the sales organization culture with non-Japanese buyers that has suppressed overseas business for 30 years has not been visibly addressed in the FY3/26 disclosure.This view will hold if the sales growth rate in Asia decelerates to less than 8% year-on-year in FY3/27, the European operating profit margin shrinks to less than 8.5%, and in addition, the American segment falls short of the expected loss cut in half set in FY3/26.
