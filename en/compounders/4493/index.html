2026-05-28 JII Compounder Profile — Initiation ↗

A cloud-WAF SaaS franchise distributing Shadan-kun and WafCharm through the AWS Marketplace, running at ARR ¥5.2bn with a 26.0% Q1 FY2026 operating margin. About 57% of ARR is AWS-linked, and the FY2030 ¥20bn sales target implies roughly 18% organic growth a year plus M&A revenue — the profile maps that arithmetic and the durability of the Q1 margin.
