2026-06-09 JII Compounder Profile — Initiation ↗

Nippon Parking Development turns underused land and idle assets into cash — car parks on an asset-light sublease model, plus Hakuba Valley ski resorts and regional theme parks. Return on equity has slid from 42% to 28% as the mix turns asset-heavier; on June 5, 2026 it launched a ¥1.0bn buyback toward a target ROE above 30%, with the stock about 20% below its September 2025 peak near 8.5x forward EV/EBIT.
