01 · Rising phase

What moved the share price over the past two years?

User Local is a SaaS company that analyzes behavior on corporate websites and comments on SNS, and also provides generated AI for corporations on a monthly basis. At the high price in May 2024, even before ChatAI's track record had been sufficiently built up, a high valuation that anticipated future growth as a generative AI company was factored into the stock price.

01 · The rally Listed in 2017, User Local has a simple structure: standalone accounts, one segment, and no subsidiaries, equity-method affiliates or goodwill. Its revenue comes from one SaaS platform with three layers: established digital marketing analytics (User Insight, Social Insight and Media Insight), AI DX SaaS (Support Chatbot and ChatAI), and free tools that lead users to paid services. More than 34 billion accumulated data records underpin model accuracy and commercial proposals. With 112 employees and one office, it generated FY25 sales of ¥4,582M and OP of ¥1,971M, a 43% margin. Strong interest in generative AI during spring and summer 2024 took the shares to ¥2,347 on May 14, 2024.

02 · The reversal The subsequent decline largely reflected multiple compression across small AI-related stocks rather than company-specific bad news. The price reached ¥1,313 on April 7, 2025, 44% below the high. FY25 earnings nevertheless grew: sales were ¥4,582M (+17.3%), OP ¥1,971M (+14.1%) and net profit ¥1,429M (+20.6%). March 2025 headquarters relocation costs slowed OP growth. FY26 guidance of ¥5,284M sales, ¥2,207M OP and ¥1,523M net profit looked modest as those costs fell away; the dividend also rose from ¥8 to ¥14. The remaining question was whether accumulating cash would fund growth, remain permanently invested conservatively, or be returned.

03 · Current position Disclosures from February to May 2026 began to answer the capital allocation question. The February 12 Q2 release showed H1 OP of ¥1,217M (+21.6%) and a first interim dividend of ¥10. On February 13, management authorized a buyback of up to 500,000 shares, or 3.12% excluding treasury shares, for up to ¥1,000M. The May 7 Q3 release showed nine-month OP of ¥1,889M (+24.4%), net profit of ¥1,384M (+31.9%) and 85.6% progress against full-year OP guidance. The final dividend increased from ¥10 to ¥14, giving ¥24 annually and a 25% payout; shareholder benefits were introduced and 100,000 treasury shares, or 0.61%, were canceled. The shares closed at ¥1,791 on May 22, 2026. Watch whether FY26 guidance is conservative or reflects timing, whether disclosure clarifies AI-DX’s contribution, and whether capital returns become an established policy.

02 · Issue

Three questions dividing investor views

Based on

's quarterly financial results and briefing materials, the points at issue can be narrowed down to three points. The question is whether profits will continue to exceed company expectations, whether ChatAI has grown to a scale commensurate with its high reputation, and whether it will continue to provide shareholder returns.

Issue 01 · Is the company planning cautious?
Are the company's FY26 forecasts conservative, or will there be a reactionary decline in profits in Q4?
Bullish For bulls, as of 9M FY26Operating income was 85.6% of the company's full-year forecast, and net income was 90.9%Point out the points that have been achieved. The head office relocation costs recorded in FY25 have been eliminated, making it easier to compare with the previous year. 9M operating profit margin is48.0%This was 280 bps higher than the same period last year. In order to meet the company's full-year profit expectations, the Q4 operating profit margin will need to fall to around 24%, but the company has not indicated that it will increase expenses by that much.This view will hold true if full-year operating income exceeds ¥2,300M.
Bearish Bearish groups will focus on the fact that approximately ¥180M's head office relocation costs were recorded at the end of FY25. Since there were no such expenses in FY26, profitability did not improve permanently as much as the 9M profit increase indicates. AI-DX sales tend to be biased in Q2 and Q3 due to the public sector's budget allocation at the beginning of the fiscal year. The reason why the company did not upwardly revise its forecasts on May 7th may be because it recognizes that its current progress is ahead of its actual ability throughout the year.This view will hold true if Q4 FY26 operating profit is less than +15% year-on-year and full-year results are within ±2% of the company's forecast ¥2,207M.
Issue 02 · AI-DX sales composition
Has ChatAI grown to a scale commensurate with its high reputation, or are most of its sales coming from conventional products?
Bullish Bulls will note that ChatAI has acquired two large customers and continues to use them.Kyoto prefecturehas been introduced to approximately 8,000 government employees,AEON grouphas expanded to more than 15 group companies. The Q2 briefing materials clearly identify both parties as current customers, and large public institutions and corporate groups tend to lead to multi-year contracts. 1H is sales+17.7%, operating profit+21.6%The profit margin for increased sales is nearly 50%. This is consistent with the view that the addition of AI-DX sales without significantly increasing existing fixed costs is pushing up company-wide profits.This view will hold true if the company discloses its recurring revenue ratio or ChatAI ARR for the first time at the FY26 financial results briefing, and if it exceeds 25% of total sales and its growth from the previous year exceeds company-wide sales.
Bearish Bears point out that the company has disclosed its results in a single segment ever since going public. One sales figure includes analysis products that have been cultivated for 15 years and generation AI products that have been available for a year, so it is difficult to say which is responsible for 9M's strong performance. It is difficult for a generative AI company to apply a high multiple without knowing the breakdown of sales. The reason why AI-DX is not disclosed separately may be because it has not yet reached a scale that would warrant independent disclosure.This view will hold true if single segment disclosure continues after FY27 and neither the recurring revenue ratio nor the number of ChatAI contracts are shown.
Issue 03 · Continuity of refund policy
Are stock buybacks in February and dividend increases in May the beginning of continued shareholder returns?
Bullish Bulls on one disclosure on May 7th3 ways to give backEmphasize the points that summarize the following. In addition to increasing the year-end dividend from ¥10 to ¥14 and raising the full-year dividend payout ratio from 16% to 25%, the company also introduced a shareholder benefit system for the first time and decided to cancel 100,000 treasury shares. These were resolved on February 13th.¥1,000M・500,000 shares repurchase limitjoin. Looking at the four movements together, it is possible that the company's shareholder return policy itself has changed, rather than being a temporary response to a decline in stock prices.This view will hold true if the FY26 financial results announcement (August 2026) clarifies the share buyback limit or the minimum dividend payout ratio of 30% or more that will continue in FY27.
Bearish Bearish groups believe that ¥1,000M's share buyback limit isApproximately 11% of ¥9.4bn of cash balanceHowever, even if the amount is fully executed, retained earnings will exceed the sum of dividends and share buybacks, so the cash level at the end of FY26 is expected to remain almost unchanged compared to the previous year. With 37.9% directly owned by the founder-CEO, this concentration of Japanese founder-led small-cap stocks has historically left them with options for acquisition opportunities that may not come.This view will hold if the FY27 capital allocation slide re-states the dividend payout ratio of ~25% without specifying the cash limit, and the FY26 share buyback ends at less than 70% of the ¥1,000M limit.
