Teddy Okuyama — Founder

Representative Director · Japan Investor Interface Co., Ltd.

US-born and Japan-based, Teddy Okuyama founded JII in 2023 after working inside more than 300 meetings between Japanese listed companies and global investors. That front-row view helps IR, CFO, and corporate-planning teams see how the market reads their company—and where clearer disclosure and dialogue can strengthen the investment case.

JII combines investor-focused diagnosis, disclosure translation, English review, and IR interpretation. Each service can stand alone or feed a continuing cycle of improvement.
