Teddy Okuyama

REPRESENTATIVE DIRECTOR

Born in the United States and based in Japan, Teddy founded Japan Investor Interface Co., Ltd. in 2023. Drawing on more than 300 meetings between Japanese companies and overseas investors, he supports English disclosure, meeting preparation, and interpretation.

For recurring clients, JII retains prior translations and terminology and provides practical IR consultation in the context of actual documents and meetings.
