IR Translation: Before and After

Was revenue up or down? Did management approve the expansion? A translation that blurs the comparison or overstates a decision can mislead investors. These before-and-after examples show where precision matters.

These examples and figures were created for comparison. No client documents or delivered translations are reproduced.

01

Revenue below plan, but up year on year

Japanese original

2027年3月期上期（2026年4～9月）の連結売上高は120億円となり、社内計画（125億円）を5億円下回りました。一方、前年同期（110億円）比では9.1％の増収でした。連結営業利益は12億円で、販管費が計画を下回ったことが寄与し、社内計画（10億円）を2億円上回りましたが、前年同期（14億円）比では14.3％の減益となりました。

Before

For the six months ended September 30, 2026, consolidated revenue was ¥12.0 billion, a ¥0.5 billion decline from the internal plan of ¥12.5 billion but up 9.1% year on year from ¥11.0 billion. Consolidated operating profit was ¥1.2 billion, a ¥0.2 billion increase from the internal plan of ¥1.0 billion but down 14.3% year on year from ¥1.4 billion. SG&A expenses came in below plan, helping operating profit exceed the planned amount.

After

For the six months ended September 30, 2026, consolidated revenue was ¥12.0 billion, ¥0.5 billion below the internal plan of ¥12.5 billion but up 9.1% year on year from ¥11.0 billion. Consolidated operating profit was ¥1.2 billion, ¥0.2 billion above the internal plan of ¥1.0 billion but down 14.3% year on year from ¥1.4 billion. SG&A expenses came in below plan, helping operating profit exceed the planned amount.

What changed

“Decline” and “increase” can sound like changes from a previous period. These amounts are differences from internal plan. “Below plan” and “above plan” state the intended benchmarks, while both versions retain the year-on-year results.

02

IFRS operating profit: ¥0.6bn; adjusted: ¥1.0bn

Japanese original

当社は、国際会計基準（IFRS）に基づく営業利益のほか、経営管理指標として調整後営業利益を開示しています。調整後営業利益は、IFRS営業利益に事業再編費用と減損損失を加え戻して算出します。2026年1～6月の連結IFRS営業利益は6億円でした。事業再編費用3億円と減損損失1億円を加え戻した調整後営業利益は10億円です。その他の調整項目はありません。

Before

We disclose adjusted operating profit as a management measure alongside operating profit under International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). Adjusted operating profit is calculated by adding back restructuring expenses and impairment losses to IFRS operating profit. For the six months ended June 30, 2026, consolidated IFRS operating profit was ¥0.6 billion. Adding back ¥0.3 billion of restructuring expenses and ¥0.1 billion of impairment losses brought operating profit to ¥1.0 billion. There were no other adjustments.

After

We disclose adjusted operating profit as a management measure alongside operating profit under International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). Adjusted operating profit is calculated by adding back restructuring expenses and impairment losses to IFRS operating profit. For the six months ended June 30, 2026, consolidated IFRS operating profit was ¥0.6 billion. Adding back ¥0.3 billion of restructuring expenses and ¥0.1 billion of impairment losses brought adjusted operating profit to ¥1.0 billion. There were no other adjustments.

What changed

The ¥1.0 billion figure is adjusted operating profit, not IFRS operating profit. The earlier wording drops “adjusted” at the decisive point. The correction makes the company-defined measure unmistakable; the full reconciliation remains identical.

03

Expansion still under review

Japanese original

Q．海外市場向けの配送拠点を1カ所新設する件について、すでに決定しているのでしょうか。また、2027年3月期の業績予想には、新拠点の影響を織り込んでいますか。

A．新拠点については現在、候補地と投資額を検討しており、新設の決定には至っていません。2027年3月期の連結営業利益予想24億円は、既存拠点の稼働率が前年並みで推移することを前提としています。新拠点の稼働による売上高と費用は、今回の予想に織り込んでいません。

Before

Q. Have you decided to open one new distribution facility to serve overseas markets? Does the forecast for the fiscal year ending March 2027 include the impact of the proposed facility?

A. We are evaluating possible sites and the required investment, and have not finalized the expansion plans. Our consolidated operating profit forecast of ¥2.4 billion for the fiscal year ending March 2027 assumes that utilization at existing facilities will remain at last year’s level. The forecast includes no revenue or costs from operating a new facility.

After

Q. Have you decided to open one new distribution facility to serve overseas markets? Does the forecast for the fiscal year ending March 2027 include the impact of the proposed facility?

A. We are evaluating possible sites and the required investment, and have not yet decided whether to open the new facility. Our consolidated operating profit forecast of ¥2.4 billion for the fiscal year ending March 2027 assumes that utilization at existing facilities will remain at last year’s level. The forecast includes no revenue or costs from operating a new facility.

What changed

“Have not finalized the expansion plans” can imply that expansion itself is settled and only the details remain. Management has not decided whether to open the facility. The correction makes that status explicit; the ¥2.4 billion forecast and exclusion of new-facility revenue and costs are unchanged.

04

Nine-month results versus a single quarter

Japanese original

2027年3月期第3四半期累計（2026年4月1日～12月31日）の連結売上高は、前年同期の165億円から9.1％増の180億円となりました。一方、10～12月の3カ月間の売上高は55億円で、前年同期の60億円を8.3％下回りました。

Before

Consolidated revenue for the third quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2027 (April 1–December 31, 2026) rose 9.1% to ¥18.0 billion from ¥16.5 billion in the same period a year earlier. Revenue for the October–December quarter alone was ¥5.5 billion, down 8.3% from ¥6.0 billion a year earlier.

After

Consolidated revenue for the first nine months of the fiscal year ending March 2027 (April 1–December 31, 2026) rose 9.1% to ¥18.0 billion from ¥16.5 billion in the same period a year earlier. Revenue for the October–December quarter alone was ¥5.5 billion, down 8.3% from ¥6.0 billion a year earlier.

What changed

The ¥18.0 billion figure covers nine months, not a standalone third quarter. The date range is present in both versions, but the earlier period label contradicts it. “First nine months” identifies the period correctly.

05

Investing activities used ¥3.0bn in cash

Japanese original

2027年3月期上期（2026年4月1日～9月30日）の投資活動によるキャッシュ・フローは30億円の支出となりました。主な内訳は、有形固定資産の取得による支出24億6,000万円、ソフトウエアの取得による支出8億4,000万円、定期預金の払戻しによる収入3億円です。前年同期も12億円の支出で、支出超過額は18億円増加しました。金額は百万円単位で集計し、本文では1,000万円未満を四捨五入して表示しています。

Before

For the six months ended September 30, 2026, investing activities generated net cash flow of ¥3.0 billion. We spent ¥2.46 billion on property, plant and equipment and ¥0.84 billion on software, partly offset by ¥0.30 billion received from time deposit withdrawals. The cash flow increased by ¥1.8 billion from ¥1.2 billion a year earlier. Amounts were compiled in millions of yen and are shown rounded to the nearest ¥10 million.

After

For the six months ended September 30, 2026, investing activities used a net ¥3.0 billion in cash. We spent ¥2.46 billion on property, plant and equipment and ¥0.84 billion on software, partly offset by ¥0.30 billion received from time deposit withdrawals. The net outflow was ¥1.8 billion larger than the ¥1.2 billion outflow a year earlier. Amounts were compiled in millions of yen and are shown rounded to the nearest ¥10 million.

What changed

The Japanese describes a net cash outflow of ¥3.0 billion, which widened by ¥1.8 billion from the previous year. “Generated” and “increased” misleadingly suggest an inflow. Both versions otherwise retain the cash-flow components and rounding convention.

06

Orders pulled forward before a price increase

Japanese original

当社は2026年7月1日、家庭用浄水器の販売価格を平均8％引き上げました。値上げを前に卸売先からの注文が6月に集中したことで、4～6月の売上高は前年同期比15.0％増の46億円となりました。一方、7～9月は前倒し発注の反動で出荷数量が減り、売上高は前年同期比10.0％減の36億円となりました。

Before

We raised prices for our household water purifiers by an average of 8% on July 1, 2026. Orders from wholesalers were concentrated in June ahead of the increase, lifting April–June revenue 15.0% year on year to ¥4.6 billion. The resulting change in order timing led to lower shipments in July–September, when revenue fell 10.0% year on year to ¥3.6 billion.

After

We raised prices for our household water purifiers by an average of 8% on July 1, 2026. Wholesalers brought orders forward into June ahead of the increase, lifting April–June revenue 15.0% year on year to ¥4.6 billion. Those advance orders reduced shipments in July–September, when revenue fell 10.0% year on year to ¥3.6 billion.

What changed

Both translations include the July 1 price increase, June orders, and the two revenue figures. The revision uses an active subject and a direct causal link between orders brought forward and lower shipments the following quarter.
