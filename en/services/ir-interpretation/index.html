JII supports investor meetings held in person in Japan and online. We identify what the investor is trying to establish and what management is trying to explain, then convey both sides accurately in the context of the figures and the business.

JII interpretation

We interpret the purpose of the question and the context of the answer.

Investors and management can use the same words while working from different premises. JII identifies what the investor is trying to establish and what management is trying to explain, then interprets the exchange consecutively without losing the financial and business context. We review the relevant materials and prior disclosure as part of that preparation.

Meeting formats

In-person meetings in Japan and online meetings

JII provides consecutive interpretation for one-on-one meetings between overseas investors and Japanese companies.

01

In-person meetings in Japan

We interpret investor meetings held in Japan after confirming the participants, materials, and main topics in advance.

02

Online meetings

We also provide consecutive interpretation for online one-on-one meetings, with the same advance review of materials and terminology.

03

Consecutive interpretation

Speakers pause at suitable intervals while we interpret each part of the exchange. We pay close attention to figures and KPIs, as well as to what the question is trying to establish and what the answer is intended to convey.

Preparation

Additional preparation and review where useful

Interpretation can be commissioned on its own. If useful, we can also review anticipated Q&A beforehand and organize the main questions after the meeting.

  • Review results materials, prior disclosure, and documents to be used in the meeting
  • Check KPIs, business terminology, and likely areas of questioning
  • Review anticipated Q&A where available
  • If requested, organize the main questions and explanation points after the meeting
Process

From the initial brief to the meeting

01

Confirm the meeting

We confirm the date, participants, whether the meeting is in person in Japan or online, its purpose, and the main topics.

02

Review the materials

We read the latest results, prior disclosure, and the documents to be used in the meeting. Anticipated Q&A and terminology lists can also be reviewed where available.

03

Interpret the meeting

We interpret the conversation consecutively, allowing each side to speak in manageable sections and paying attention to both the substance and nuance of the exchange.

04

Review if requested

If requested, we organize the main questions that came up and points that may merit clearer explanation in future meetings or disclosure.
