FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we book interpretation only?
Yes. We recommend a 30-minute pre-briefing and 5-minute debrief so the meeting also improves future IR.
Do you bring simultaneous-interpretation equipment?
Clients normally arrange venue equipment. We advise on setup, check the configuration, and can introduce vendors.
Do you travel overseas?
Yes. We quote by itinerary and have supported meetings in Hong Kong, Singapore, London, and New York.
Confidentiality?
Each engagement is covered by an NDA. Meeting content stays with the parties.
Can you take a meeting on short notice?
Send the schedule, participants, format, and materials. One to two weeks is ideal, but urgent requests are considered.
