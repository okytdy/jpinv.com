SERVICE / IR INTERPRETATION

Interpreters who understand the IR room raise the resolution of the meeting.

For institutional-investor 1-on-1s, earnings briefings, IR Days, shareholder meetings, overseas roadshows, and conference meetings, an interpreter with real IR-room experience sits in. Unlike general interpretation, we connect the investor's question intent, the temperature of the CEO/CFO's words, and IR-specific vocabulary — capital allocation, unit economics — accurately in both directions.

WHY IR-EXPERIENCED INTERPRETERS

What is different from general interpretation

General interpretation guarantees linguistic accuracy. IR interpretation adds accuracy of meaning in the investor frame — the priority order of capital allocation, the confidence level of forward statements, the context of each KPI, the governance narrative. A mistranslation here directly affects an investor's judgment. Before every session we reconcile terminology with the tanshin, the presentation deck, and past disclosure so the spoken English matches the written English.
INTERPRETATION SCOPE

Where we interpret

01

Institutional 1-on-1s

Consecutive interpretation. In a 30-minute meeting we keep the tempo, read question intent correctly, and pass numbers, KPIs, and guidance through without over-paraphrasing.

02

Earnings briefings

Simultaneous (booth) or consecutive. Presentation and Q&A handled differently; terminology aligned with the tanshin and deck; audio designed for webcast and recording.

03

IR Day / Capital Markets Day

Multiple speakers and sessions without the explanation drifting — prepared against the mid-term plan, integrated report, and past Q&A.

04

Conference meetings

Back-to-back meetings at broker conferences with stable vocabulary across sessions. Online and hybrid formats supported.

05

Overseas roadshows

Accompanied engagements to Hong Kong, Singapore, London, New York and other hubs. Travel-with, local-hire, and online-mix formats designed per trip.

06

Shareholder meetings

English support that does not break the chair's flow — agenda items, business report, and shareholder Q&A, with vocabulary matched to the translated convocation notice.

MEETING SUPPORT

Turning meetings into an IR learning loop

Around the interpretation itself, we can add investor-frame anticipated Q&A, script preparation for the CEO/CFO, and post-meeting response analysis — so the meeting feeds the next disclosure instead of ending when the call ends.
  • Anticipated Q&A list by investor frame: capital allocation / unit economics / governance / forward guidance
  • Pre-meeting briefing (30 minutes, 1–2 weeks ahead): materials, Q&A, investor profile
  • 5-minute debrief immediately after: investor reaction temperature, items needing clarification
  • Meeting observation report (optional): top 3–5 investor reactions and follow-up items, anonymized

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we book interpretation only?
Yes — though we recommend the minimum bundle of a 30-minute pre-briefing and a 5-minute debrief. That is what upgrades the output from linguistic accuracy to accuracy in the investor frame.
Do you bring simultaneous-interpretation equipment?
Venue equipment is normally arranged by the client; we advise on setup and can introduce vendors, and we check the venue configuration in advance.
Do you travel overseas?
Yes. We quote per itinerary; we have accompanied engagements to Hong Kong, Singapore, London, and New York.
Confidentiality?
Individual NDA on every engagement. Nothing said or observed in a meeting is shared outside the parties.
Can you take a meeting on short notice?
Send the schedule, participants, format, and materials — we confirm feasibility quickly. Ideal preparation starts 1–2 weeks out, but we accommodate urgent requests where possible.
