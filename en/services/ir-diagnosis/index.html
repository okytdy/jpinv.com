Purpose

Beyond correct English:
is the investment case explained?

Grammatically correct disclosure can still leave capital allocation, earnings structure, or growth assumptions unclear. We identify missing explanations and inconsistencies across materials.

Criteria

12 items, two points each, 24 points in total

The score is accompanied by findings and a clear order of improvement.

Deliverables

Findings your internal teams can use

Priorities are framed for IR, the CFO office, corporate planning, and management. Standard delivery is within ten business days after receiving the materials.

  • One-page diagnosis card: score and three priorities
  • 10–20 page report: rationale, issues, and recommendations
  • 30–60 minute briefing on the result and next actions

FAQ

Before you inquire

How is this different from a translation check?
In addition to English quality, we assess whether the information an investor needs is clearly communicated.
What materials should we prepare?
The latest Japanese and English earnings release and presentation, plus the English IR website.
Can you review a single document?
Yes. The scope can be limited to a results deck, integrated report, or timely disclosure.
