WHY DIAGNOSTIC, NOT TRANSLATION

We assess the investment case, not just the English.

Correct English can still leave investors unsure about capital allocation, growth drivers, or management intent. The diagnostic identifies those gaps across disclosure, the IR site, and meeting Q&A.
DIAGNOSTIC SCOPE — 12 ITEMS

What we score — 12 items, 24 points

Each item is scored 0–2 (24 points total). The balance across items and the written comments matter more than the absolute score.
01

Capital allocation narrative

Do cash, capex, returns, and M&A priorities tell one consistent story?

02

Unit economics / core driver

Can investors see the economics that drive growth and returns?

03

KPI consistency

Are definitions and figures consistent across every channel?

04

Forward guidance temperature

Does the wording convey management’s actual level of confidence?

05

Management commentary

Does management sound accountable for capital allocation and outcomes?

06

Governance narrative

Can global investors understand board, pay, and succession choices?

07

Segment granularity

Can investors see how each business creates value?

08

Risk disclosure specificity

Specific company risks—or generic boilerplate?

09

ESG / sustainability context

Is sustainability connected to strategy and value creation?

10

Investor-frame phrasing

Are commitments, caveats, and claims clear to global investors?

11

Meeting / interpretation design

Do preparation and scripts anticipate how investors will ask?

12

IR site readability

Can global investors find and use what they need?

DELIVERABLES

A shared agenda for the next quarter.

A one-page diagnosis card and 10–20-page commentary give IR, the CFO office, corporate planning, and management one set of priorities. First-time diagnostics are normally delivered within 10 business days.
  • Diagnosis card: score, top three priorities, and benchmark notes
  • Commentary: rationale, evidence, and direction for each item
  • Marked-up materials for the top priorities, as needed
  • Investor Q&A skeletons, as needed
  • 30–60-minute debrief; follow-up questions for two weeks

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a translation vendor's check?
A language check fixes the draft. The diagnostic tests whether the investment case is clear and complete.
Which companies is this for?
Listed companies building meaningful dialogue with global investors, regardless of market segment.
What materials should we prepare?
Latest Japanese and English results materials, IR site, meeting scripts, and integrated report, where available.
How long does it take?
Normally within 10 business days. Results-season timing is available by prior arrangement.
Can you also produce the materials?
Yes. Translation, review, meeting support, and interpretation are available separately. See the services page.
Confidentiality?
Each engagement is covered by an NDA. Findings stay private; any case reference is anonymized.
