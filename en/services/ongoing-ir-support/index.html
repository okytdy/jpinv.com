SERVICE / ONGOING IR SUPPORT

Sharpening the dialogue with overseas investors, quarter by quarter.

Not a one-off diagnosis or review, but a retainer that improves the overseas-investor dialogue on a quarterly cycle: English IR review before each results date, meeting preparation and debriefs, continuous disclosure improvement, and a cumulative anticipated-Q&A file — four cycles a year plus ad hoc. Because we retain the company context and equity story, you never re-explain the business from zero.

Request a quote
IMPROVEMENT LOOP

One loop from diagnosis to meetings

When translation, interpretation, and review are procured separately, no understanding of the company accumulates. The retainer runs these six stages as one flow.
01

IR diagnosis / diagnosis card

The starting point: current state scored on 12 items / 24 points, priorities on one card.

02

Internal walkthrough

The card read through with IR, the CFO office, corporate planning, and management so next quarter's priorities are shared.

03

Equity story design

Investment highlights, growth assumptions, profitability, capital allocation, and competitive advantage organized into one story told the same way everywhere.

04

Quarterly disclosure improvement

The agreed priorities reflected into the next earnings deck, English disclosure, and IR-site update on the same axis.

05

Meeting prep & Q&A

The sensitive topics — capital allocation, shareholder returns, growth assumptions, governance — turned into short, answerable Q&A.

06

Post-meeting review

Investor questions and reactions recorded and fed back into the next disclosure and Q&A update.

DELIVERABLES

What you receive each quarter

  • Quarterly IR-readiness report: pre-results English IR check and improvement priorities
  • Meeting observation notes: anonymized observations from overseas-investor meetings
  • Cumulative anticipated-Q&A updates reflecting past investor reactions
  • Monthly 30-minute sync with IR / corporate planning / CFO (1–2 per month)
  • Ad hoc consultation during the contract period

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum term?
Six months (two quarters). One quarter is not enough for the communication changes to take hold, so we recommend at least two cycles.
Which companies is this for?
Listed companies with meaningful overseas ownership, or planning to build overseas-investor reach. Market segment does not matter.
Can we start with a single translation or meeting instead?
Yes. No retainer required — start with one tanshin or one meeting and judge the quality first.
Can we take just the diagnosis first?
Yes. Light (initial diagnosis) is the entry point; decide on continuation after seeing the results.
