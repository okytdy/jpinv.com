AI use

Agree the scope before work begins

We confirm whether AI will be used and for which tasks. Whenever it is used, a person reviews the final translation and findings.

01

Prior agreement

We agree the purpose, scope, settings, and approval requirements for any external service.

02

Limited inputs

Information is anonymized where appropriate and limited to what the task requires.

03

Human final review

We verify figures, comparisons, causality, and notes before anything is ready for disclosure.

NDA & MNPI

Set the conditions before materials arrive.

We can execute an NDA before receiving documents, using either party's form. Unpublished information is used only for the agreed work, with the materials, purpose, and responsible person clearly defined.

  • Access to unpublished information is restricted to assigned personnel
  • Meeting content is not shared beyond the engagement team
  • Any case example is anonymized and does not identify the company
Data retention

90 days after delivery, as a rule

Our standard retention period is 90 days after delivery. Nonpublic information and contact details are used only as required for the engagement.

90DAYS / STANDARD
  • Any different retention or deletion requirement is agreed before work begins
  • When prior translations or glossaries are retained, the scope and purpose are documented
FAQ

Before you inquire

Do you use AI on every engagement?
No. We agree whether and how it may be used for each engagement. A person reviews every final deliverable.
Must I send materials before an NDA is signed?
No. Where needed, please send the materials only after the NDA is in place.
