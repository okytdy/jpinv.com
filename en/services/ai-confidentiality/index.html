POLICY / AI & CONFIDENTIALITY

AI usage policy and confidentiality controls

Because we handle unreleased materials and the contents of investor meetings, the scope of AI use, receipt of materials, working scope, and post-delivery retention are confirmed per engagement. This page consolidates JII's AI policy and confidentiality practice.

Request a quote
01 / AI USAGE

Scope of AI use

Whether and how AI is used is confirmed in advance per engagement, based on the work, the client's requirements, and the agreed information-handling terms. Where AI assists, it operates inside the agreed confidentiality, retention, and human-review rules — final translations and review judgments are made by a person.
  • Purpose, scope, and settings confirmed before any unreleased or personal information touches an external service
  • Anonymization, summarization, or input restriction applied where needed
  • Handling restrictions and prior-approval requirements confirmed at the inquiry stage
02 / NDA & UNRELEASED INFORMATION

NDA workflow and MNPI handling

Where needed, an NDA is signed before materials are received (our template or yours). Unreleased results, pre-release drafts, and meeting contents are handled only to the extent the work requires, with materials, purpose, and personnel defined per engagement.
  • Access to unreleased information restricted per engagement
  • Nothing said or observed in meetings is shared outside the parties
  • Case references, if any, anonymized to industry, size, and issue type
03 / DATA RETENTION

Data retention

Post-delivery data retention is 90 days as a rule. Non-public information is never used for anything other than the engagement. Contact details and meeting-participant information are handled only as needed to perform the work.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you use AI on every engagement?
No. Whether and how AI is used depends on the work, your requirements, and the agreed information-handling terms. Final deliverables are checked by a person.
Do we need to send materials before the NDA?
No. Materials can be shared after the NDA is in place.
