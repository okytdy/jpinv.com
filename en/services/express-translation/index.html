Frequently asked questions

Before you contact us

Can you deliver today?
It depends on the document type, volume and receipt time. An earnings-release summary may be completed within a few hours.
Can you start before the Japanese version is finished?
Yes. We can begin from a near-final draft and incorporate changes in the final version.
Can we commission review of an AI-translated draft only?
Yes. We check figures, subjects, reasons for changes, comparisons and notes.
How do you handle unpublished earnings information?
An NDA is signed where required, and the scope of any AI use is confirmed in advance.
How is the price determined?
We quote according to document type, volume, deadline and scope. An urgent-work surcharge may apply in addition to the standard rate.Pricing page for further details.
