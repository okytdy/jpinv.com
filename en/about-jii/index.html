About JII

Disclosure leads to a meeting. The meeting shows what to improve.

Japan Investor Interface Co., Ltd. — JII — handles English disclosure, interpretation for meetings with overseas investors, and the work of improving that disclosure, for Japanese listed companies.

ContactPricing
One cycle

The three are one piece of work

Each circle links to that service

English disclosure is the starting point for dialogue with overseas investors. The questions investors raise reveal not only what matters to them, but also where the disclosure is incomplete or open to misunderstanding. JII treats translation, meeting interpretation and post-meeting review as connected parts of the same IR process, turning what is learned in dialogue into clearer disclosure and better-prepared meetings.
