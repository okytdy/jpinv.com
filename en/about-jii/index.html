The overseas IR JII works toward

Connect disclosureand dialogue tobetter management.

Speaking English is not enough to make an investor meeting productive. A company needs to frame its strengths, growth strategy, and capital allocation around the questions an investor must answer.

JII combines English disclosure, IR interpretation, and diagnosis so that investor reactions and doubts improve the next management decision and disclosure cycle.

The corporate-value cycle

Disclose. Meet. Return the learning.

The goal is not to publish English or hold meetings. It is to communicate the business and management's intent, then connect investor questions to better decisions and clearer disclosure.
