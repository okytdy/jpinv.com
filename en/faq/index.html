Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before you engage us. If yours is not here, send a note.

Request a quote
What can we bring to you?
Disclosure translation and review, IR interpretation, investor-focused diagnosis, and ongoing support. Start with one document, one meeting, or a quarterly cycle.
Can we start with just one job?
Yes. Start with one disclosure, deck, or meeting. No retainer is required.
Can you pick up where another vendor left off?
Yes. We review existing translations, glossaries, and past materials, then keep what works and fix what does not.
Can we commission review only?
Yes. We review in-house, vendor, or AI drafts for accuracy, consistency, and investor readability.
Do you handle same-day or short-deadline work?
It depends on type, volume, release time, and draft status. We confirm feasibility before accepting same-day or next-business-day work.
What should we share before an interpretation job?
Purpose, participants, format, materials, and expected topics. Past disclosure and anticipated Q&A help keep spoken and written IR consistent.
Simultaneous, consecutive, or online?
All three. Simultaneous often suits briefings and IR Days; consecutive often suits 1-on-1s. We advise by format.
How is the IR diagnosis different from translation or review?
Translation improves a document. Diagnosis finds where the investment case loses clarity across disclosure, the IR site, and meeting Q&A. See IR diagnosis & review.
How is pricing set?
Translation is per source character or word; interpretation by half-day or day; ongoing support is fixed-fee. See the pricing page.
How do you handle unreleased materials and AI tools?
We agree NDA, AI use, access, and retention per engagement. A person remains responsible for final work. See AI & confidentiality.
