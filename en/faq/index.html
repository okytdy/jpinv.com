FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often before an engagement. Anything not covered here — use the contact form.

Request a quote
What can we bring to you?
Translation of tanshin, timely disclosures, AGM notices, annual securities reports, earnings decks, integrated reports, and IR websites — plus interpretation for overseas investor 1-on-1s, earnings briefings, IR Days, shareholder meetings, and roadshows. Start with a single document or meeting, or a quarterly retainer.
Can we start with just one job?
Yes. No retainer required. Try one tanshin, one deck, or one meeting and judge the quality, process, and responsiveness first.
Can you pick up where another vendor left off?
Yes. If you have existing translations, glossaries, or past materials, we work from them — first mapping inconsistencies, then deciding what to inherit and what to fix. We do not insist on starting over.
Can we commission review only?
Yes. For in-house, vendor, or AI-translated English we check terminology consistency, overseas investor readability, drift from past materials, and anything that reads oddly as disclosure.
Do you handle same-day or short-deadline work?
It depends on document type, volume, release time, and whether a draft translation exists. Same-day / next-business-day delivery runs through the express translation service (page in Japanese). We confirm feasibility before accepting.
What should we share before an interpretation job?
Purpose, participants, format, materials, and expected topics. The earnings deck, past English disclosure, anticipated questions, and any glossary let us match the spoken English to your written English.
Simultaneous, consecutive, or online?
All three. Large-audience settings like earnings briefings and IR Days often suit simultaneous; 1-on-1s and small meetings often run better consecutive, where question intent can be confirmed. We advise per format.
How is the IR diagnosis different from translation or review?
Translation and review fix the document in front of you. The diagnosis reads across materials, the IR site, and meeting Q&A to find where overseas investors stop understanding. See IR diagnosis & review.
How is pricing set?
Translation is per character (JP→EN ¥6–14/char), interpretation per half-day or day (from ¥50,000/half-day), retainers monthly (Light ¥200,000/engagement, Standard ¥100,000/month, Pro ¥200,000/month). See the pricing page.
How do you handle unreleased materials and AI tools?
NDA as needed; unreleased materials are handled only to the extent the work requires. AI use and scope are agreed per engagement, with final judgment by a person. Post-delivery retention is 90 days. See AI & confidentiality.
