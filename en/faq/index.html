FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions clients usually ask before a first assignment. If yours is not here, send us a note.

What work can JII take on?
English translation and review for disclosure materials, interpretation for investor meetings, ongoing translation, and practical IR advice tied to a real document or meeting. You can start with one assignment.
Can we start with just one job?
Yes. Start with one disclosure, deck, or meeting. No retainer is required.
Can you pick up where another vendor left off?
Yes. We review existing translations, glossaries, and past materials, then keep what works and fix what does not.
Can we commission review only?
Yes. We review in-house, vendor, or AI drafts for accuracy, consistency, and investor readability.
Can you handle same-day or next-business-day work?
It depends on the document type, volume, release time, and draft status. We confirm feasibility before accepting urgent work.
What should we share before an interpretation job?
Purpose, participants, format, materials, and expected topics. Past disclosure and anticipated Q&A help keep spoken and written IR consistent.
Do you offer simultaneous, consecutive, and online interpretation?
Yes. Simultaneous interpretation often suits briefings and IR Days; consecutive interpretation often suits one-on-one meetings. We recommend a format based on the meeting.
Can we ask for IR advice alongside a translation?
Yes. We can discuss wording, explanation order, and likely investor questions using the actual document or meeting as the starting point.
How is pricing set?
Translation is based on source characters or words and interpretation on booked time. Ongoing work is quoted per assignment unless a recurring volume has been agreed. See the pricing page.
How do you handle unreleased materials and AI tools?
The tools used and the applicable information-handling conditions are confirmed in advance for unpublished materials. Teddy Okuyama reviews every final deliverable in full. See Quality Control and Confidentiality.
