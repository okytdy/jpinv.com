About the portfolio

I’m Teddy Okuyama. This free, personal project follows my research through simulated investments, starting with ¥10 million on 2 November 2026.

Entries use the day’s volume-weighted average price (VWAP), plus a modeled 0.2% cost. Whole shares are rounded down; the rest stays in cash. Returns include cash, recorded costs and dividends. Prices are dated, not live.

This is a learning record, not investment advice or an actual funded account.

Prices and costs

VWAP is the session’s traded value divided by traded volume. Purchases add 0.2%; sales deduct 0.2%. This is an illustrative all-in cost, not a broker quotation. Entry details retain the reference prices and amounts. A reference-priced entry may be recorded after its market date.

Weights and returns

Weights use the latest saved closing prices. Average cost includes purchase costs. Portfolio return compares total holdings plus cash with contributed hypothetical capital. Dividends appear only when recorded; no forecast income or cash interest is assumed. Whole shares, including odd lots, are permitted.

Data and project

Prices come from TSE session records. JPX daily market data ↗. This is a saved snapshot, updated manually. The personal publication and portfolio are free and will never be monetized. They are separate from Japan Investor Interface Co., Ltd. and its professional services.
