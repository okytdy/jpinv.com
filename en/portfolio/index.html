How this portfolio works

This is my personal research project, shared alongside the Substack. It helps me revisit decisions and prepare for investing when I have surplus savings to commit for the long term. This publication and its paper portfolio are free and will never be monetized.

The publication is separate from my professional work through Japan Investor Interface Co., Ltd.

Forecast models · Company website

Whole shares, including odd lots

Holdings use whole shares. Quantities below Japan’s usual 100-share trading unit are allowed. A purchase uses the largest whole-share quantity within its allocation budget, including modeled transaction costs; the remainder stays in cash.

VWAP and the 0.2% cost assumption

The reference entry price is the chosen session’s volume-weighted average price (VWAP). Purchases add 0.2%; sales deduct 0.2%. This single hypothetical cost covers transaction friction for this simulation and is not a broker quotation.

The first decision was recorded after the November 2 close using that day’s full-session VWAP. It is a reference-priced simulation, not a claim that an order could have been filled at that price.

Performance, cash and dividends

Portfolio value equals cash plus holdings at their dated closing prices. Return since inception is the change from the original ¥10 million, after recorded transaction costs and including cash. Cash earns no interest. Verified dividends are credited to cash when paid; taxes are not modeled. No dividends have been recorded at launch.

Positions are marked at the close, without deducting a hypothetical future selling cost. A sale incurs its cost when recorded. Splits and other corporate actions require explicit ledger adjustments. No new external cash is assumed after inception.

Dates, sources and updates

This is a dated end-of-day record. The “Prices as of” date identifies the saved valuation; the page is not a live quote feed. Allocation dates and original entry references stay in the activity history as later observations are added.
