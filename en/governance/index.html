KNOWLEDGE CHECK

Check your starting point.

Three questions cover ideas that are essential to the overall reform story.

Q1 / ORIGINS OF REFORM

Which document is commonly cited as the source of the “8% ROE” reference point for Japanese companies?

The Ito Review, published by METI in August 2014, identified 8% ROE as a possible threshold above global investors' expected cost of equity.

Q2 / CAPITAL EFFICIENCY

Which idea is not part of the TSE's request for management conscious of cost of capital and share price?

PBR is not the target itself; it is an outcome of the market's assessment.

Q3 / ENGLISH DISCLOSURE

When did simultaneous Japanese–English timely disclosure become mandatory for Prime Market companies?

The requirement took effect on 1 April 2025.
