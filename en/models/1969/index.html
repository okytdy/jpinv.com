Key Assumptions

  • Sales: ¥440bn in FY3/27, then ¥463bn, ¥487bn and ¥512bn. The starting year is company guidance; later sales growth of roughly 5% annually is JII’s assumption.
  • Margins: OP margin rises from 11.36% to 11.50%, 11.70% and 11.80%. This improvement is a JII estimate; the model does not separately quantify annual pricing, staffing and cost changes. Failure to achieve it would reduce forecast EPS.
  • EPS: net income stays at 80% of OP, matching the FY3/27 guidance ratio. Weighted shares remain 130.877m. EPS growth therefore comes from the assumed sales growth and margin improvement, with no contribution from future buybacks.
