Key Assumptions

  • Delivery: FY3/27 uses available backlog and incoming orders with the prior H2 financial recognition rate of 35.79%. Later real throughput grows 4% annually; price/mix adds 2%. These are financial proxies, not physical capacity disclosures.
  • Costs: direct unit costs rise 2%; pay rises 5% with staffing up 2%. Other overhead has 3% inflation and 2% scope growth. Historical H2 expense seasonality is retained. These budgets determine margins; no margin path is imposed.
  • EPS: prospective tax is 32.29%, with separate minority claims and no unexecuted buybacks. The tax treatment of identified exceptional items remains a material sensitivity.
  • Funding: seasonal working capital, tax/bonus payments and dividend timing require ¥13.80bn of new FY3/27 borrowing in the base. This funding is uncommitted; slower collections or less financing would change the outcome.
