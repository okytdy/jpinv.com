Azoom key assumptions and rationale
The central case permits gradual improvement while charging payroll, software, security and repricing delays. FY30 EPS is ¥564.35 and ordinary DPS ¥250.50. These are JII estimates; the prior ¥635.32/¥270.21 case is superseded.
Tenant repricing takes time
“Full yen recovery” means an eventual extra ¥1 of tenant-service receipts for each extra ¥1 of owner cost per space, after adjusting for occupancy. It does not mean a 100% tenant rent increase or preservation of percentage margins. Owner rent on more spaces is a volume cost and is not passed through again as inflation.
Management’s FY26 briefing
says existing-owner hikes concentrated in Q4 before tenant revisions. It expects existing-stock inflation to be approximately neutral in FY27 and maintains gross profit on new inventory. It does not disclose achieved recovery, same-property rent growth, renegotiation duration or a future multi-year guarantee.
JII rationale:
eventual recovery is supported by this current commercial outlook. Immediate full recovery contradicts the observed sequence. The base therefore recognizes half of each new annual unit-cost increase in-year and half next year, a JII half-year average negotiation allowance. This is not an issuer timetable. Annual owner unit-cost growth of 3% includes location/mix as well as repricing; assuming it can all be recovered remains conditional. No unmeasured FY26 catch-up or separate fee-price premium is added.

When IT benefits begin to affect earnings
Already achieved in FY26:
SYNAPSE cut contract-administration time by more than half. Management says the released time did not yet generate sufficient additional contracts. More than 100 recruits diluted experience.
Expected by management in FY27H2:
staff maturation and further sales-guidance AI should improve conversion. This is management’s expectation, not an achieved benefit or committed rollout date. A H1 nearFY26 group throughput17.85 and H2 around19.15 illustrates the modest FY27 annual18.5 allowance; it is not a separate quarterly forecast. FY28 is the first full-year carry-through in JII’s gradual path. No portion of EPS is uniquely attributed to AI.

house_assumption
FY26 Q4 owner repricing preceded tenant repricing. In currentFY27 management expects existing-stock cost inflation to be offset, without quantified recovery achieved or timing. New stock is selected for gross profit; no multi-year matched-cohort series disclosed.
Retain100% eventual yen-level recovery as the commercial anchor, but recognize50% of each annual owner-unit-cost increment in the year it arises and the other50% next year. This is an approximately six-month lag proxy if increases arise around the year start and tenant adjustments phase through the year. It is an annual convention, not a measured contract-renewal schedule. No percentage-margin preservation, discretionary mix uplift or unquantified prior-year catch-up.
The actual cost-before-price sequence disproves automatic simultaneous pass-through, while management’s concrete FY27 outlook supports recovery rather than permanent arbitrary spread erosion. A six-month central recognition delay represents ongoing negotiation and tenant-change opportunities; it is a house estimate bracketed by immediate and twelve-month delay tests. Eventual full recovery across future aggregate mix remains conditional and is tested at 75%,50%,0%. No contractual automatic escalator or guarantee is claimed.
Half current-year increment plus half prior-year increment; first year excludes an unmeasured FY26 catch-up. Fully effective level recovery follows one year later, not full same-year earnings recovery. New cohorts are priced through the same aggregate proxy, a material simplification.
Immediate full recovery;75% eventual recovery with six-month lag;50% eventual with lag;zero recovery;100% eventual with twelve-month lag. Bear combines50% eventual with lag and other weaker operating inputs.
Existing-tenant rent revision fails to match owner increases over subsequent renewals; churn rises when rents increase; new inventory economics deteriorate; achieved pricing evidence requires a different lag.
