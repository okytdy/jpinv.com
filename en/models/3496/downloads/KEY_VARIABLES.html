Azoom key assumptions and rationale

The central case permits gradual improvement while charging payroll, software, security and repricing delays. FY30 EPS is ¥564.35 and ordinary DPS ¥250.50. These are JII estimates; the prior ¥635.32/¥270.21 case is superseded.

Tenant repricing takes time

“Full yen recovery” means an eventual extra ¥1 of tenant-service receipts for each extra ¥1 of owner cost per space, after adjusting for occupancy. It does not mean a 100% tenant rent increase or preservation of percentage margins. Owner rent on more spaces is a volume cost and is not passed through again as inflation.

Management’s FY26 briefing says existing-owner hikes concentrated in Q4 before tenant revisions. It expects existing-stock inflation to be approximately neutral in FY27 and maintains gross profit on new inventory. It does not disclose achieved recovery, same-property rent growth, renegotiation duration or a future multi-year guarantee.

JII rationale: eventual recovery is supported by this current commercial outlook. Immediate full recovery contradicts the observed sequence. The base therefore recognizes half of each new annual unit-cost increase in-year and half next year, a JII half-year average negotiation allowance. This is not an issuer timetable. Annual owner unit-cost growth of 3% includes location/mix as well as repricing; assuming it can all be recovered remains conditional. No unmeasured FY26 catch-up or separate fee-price premium is added.

When IT benefits begin to affect earnings

Already achieved in FY26: SYNAPSE cut contract-administration time by more than half. Management says the released time did not yet generate sufficient additional contracts. More than 100 recruits diluted experience.

Expected by management in FY27H2: staff maturation and further sales-guidance AI should improve conversion. This is management’s expectation, not an achieved benefit or committed rollout date. A H1 nearFY26 group throughput17.85 and H2 around19.15 illustrates the modest FY27 annual18.5 allowance; it is not a separate quarterly forecast. FY28 is the first full-year carry-through in JII’s gradual path. No portion of EPS is uniquely attributed to AI.

Investment clock: opening software construction in progress¥423m is commissioned70%/30% in FY27/28 for depreciation purposes. Software/equipment spending¥550/610/670/730m and continuing platform/security expenses fund the build. Commissioning is a JII accounting convention, not the productivity start date. Software uses four-year and new equipment six-year lives with half-year first-year depreciation. No additional AI payroll-saving overlay is counted.
