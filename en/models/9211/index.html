Key Assumptions

  • Growth: Q4 activity rises 2.5% from the Q3 financial base, then 6% annually; price/mix adds 1.5% annually. Existing consolidated businesses are retained, with no new M&A.
  • Costs: variable inputs rise 2.5% and fixed cash costs 5.5%; 60% of the cost pool responds to activity. No future unclassified operating gain is repeated. The separate tax treatment of valuation income and impairment materially affects starting EPS.
  • Seller obligations: the ¥4.79bn aggregate liability is an imperfect cash-payment proxy. The ¥750m fixed AI ONE price is separately funded as assumed unpaid. Actual settlement status, terms and earnings ownership remain uncertain.
  • Funding and EPS: the base assumes borrowing can return to the observed ¥12.32bn ceiling; this is uncommitted. Tighter capacity causes dilution. Minority-buyout accretion is excluded until earnings ownership is evidenced, and appears only as a conditional sensitivity.
