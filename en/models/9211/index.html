Assumptions

  • Growth: H2 activity rises 6%, then 8%/6.5%/5% annually. Existing acquisitions are annualized; no new M&A is assumed.
  • Margins: price/mix improves 1.5%, versus 2.5% variable-input inflation and 5.5% fixed-cost growth. Slower activity growth leaves less cost absorption, easing later margins.
  • Seller obligations: puts settle 0%/20%/30%/50% of the original covered pool; earnouts settle 10%/25%/30%/35%. Payment amounts track assumed acquired-company earnings. The ¥750m fixed AI ONE price is treated as additional. Actual exercise terms and ownership mapping remain uncertain.
  • Funding and EPS: seller payments reduce cash while settling covered minority claims increases parent earnings. Refinancing is uncommitted and limited to repayments; additional need triggers discounted equity issuance. Tax is 30%; no future liability-revaluation gains are included. These funding and payment assumptions make the forecast particularly uncertain.
