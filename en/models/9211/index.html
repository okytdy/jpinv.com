Sources and calculation basis

Primary evidence: H1 FY12/26 statutory report, H1 results statements, FY12/25 annual securities report and H1 results presentation. Exact locators are retained in the download. The calculation uses existing service activity and acquisition-scope annualization, with explicit seller, minority, customer-cash and funding bridges.

Principal uncertainty: put-to-entity mapping, strike formulas, exercise timing and the ¥750m fixed-price classification remain unresolved. Fair values are conditional cash anchors, not contractual caps. Partial-period subsidiary earnings are weak annual proxies. New credit and equity are not committed; consolidated cash is not wholly free for distribution.

The October article used FY12/26 company-guided EPS ¥155.62 and an illustrative 10× multiple to produce ¥1,556. That historical valuation is not generated or updated by this independent recurring-EPS model. The published article remains the dated research narrative; this page records the later conditional calculations.
