Key Assumptions

  • Fees: a 2% management-fee proxy follows the latest quarter’s implied rate. Existing fee exposure declines as funds harvest; fixed cash costs rise 7.12% annually, reducing fee earnings before new fundraising.
  • Successor fund: the base assumes an unannounced ¥250bn fund closes in 2029 and earns half-year fees. Without it, FY12/29 fee-only EPS is about ¥18.53 instead of ¥63.37. This is the key conditional assumption.
  • Carry: additional realizations of ¥1bn in Q4, then ¥4bn/¥6bn/¥7bn, draw on the ¥23.2bn unrealized pool. Investment marks are flat; neither carry timing nor returns are recurring fee income.
  • Funding: annual net investment cash use is ¥6.5bn before successor-fund calls. The base needs ¥0.36bn of uncommitted borrowing in 2029. Cash after the deferred-tax reserve is negative; displayed cash is not distributable surplus.
