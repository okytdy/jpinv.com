Assumptions

  • Fees: a 1.95% management-fee proxy applies to fund commitments and declining older-fund fee bases. Harvesting-fund fee exposure falls from ¥86bn to ¥26bn by 2029; fixed costs rise 7.12% annually, initially squeezing fee earnings.
  • Fundraising: an unannounced ¥250bn successor fund is assumed to close during 2029, contributing half-year fees. Without it, FY12/29 fee-only EPS falls from ¥60.12 to ¥16.33.
  • Carry: assumed additional realizations of ¥1bn in H2 2026, then ¥4bn/¥6bn/¥7bn, draw on the disclosed ¥22bn unrealized pool. Timing is uncertain; carry is separate from recurring fees.
  • Cash and EPS: principal investment marks are flat; GP calls and investment returns drive cash. Tax is 30.6%, basic shares 34.067m, with separate minority claims and no buybacks. Cash is not wholly available for distribution.
