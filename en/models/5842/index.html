Latest financial anchors are dated June 30, 2026. Fee-AUM, management fees and expense facts support the operating forecast; carried interest and principal investments have separate earnings and cash schedules. FY12/26 cash-flow fields in the full model cover H2 only. The download identifies the actuals, JII assumptions, source passages and old-to-new reconciliation.

Principal uncertainty: no successor fund has been announced in the verified sources. Its size and timing, harvesting-fund fee run-off and investment capital returns are JII assumptions. Carry timing is low confidence; realization is bounded by the disclosed June pool, with a separate compensation assumption. The fee-only definition is a house normalization; investment-entity accounting and minority claims remain explicit.
