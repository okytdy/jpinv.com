Key Assumptions

  • Revenue: existing center/trucking activity is flat. Center wins add 4.5% of prior-year sales against 1% churn; center/trucking prices rise 2%/2.2%. These consolidated financial proxies do not isolate Gunma’s additional consolidation contribution.
  • Costs: wages rise 3.5%, other variable inputs 1.5%; center/truck productivity improves 1.4%/1.7%. The identified ¥263m acquisition fee is removed once from recurring earnings and the cost anchor. Ordinary depreciation and acquisition goodwill have separate schedules.
  • Investment: dated construction-payment estimates leave ¥9.63bn of named-project cash after September. Actual payment progress is undisclosed; full building costs still enter depreciation when commissioned. Maintenance and discretionary spending remain separate.
  • EPS and funding: tax is 33.5%, with separate minority claims and no future buybacks. September cash equivalents are ¥23.62bn; bank repayments are ¥3.5bn annually. Operating rents remain expensed; these are economic forecasts before the new lease standard.
