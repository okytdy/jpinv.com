Sources and calculation basis

Primary evidence: FY3/26 results; H1 FY3/27 results,30 October 2026; FY3/26 annual securities report; and FY3/26 supplemental presentation. Exact locators and observed-versus-assumed input classifications are retained in the assumption register.

The company plans to adopt the new lease standard from April 2027 and has not quantified its effect. These recurring economic estimates retain operating rents in costs and separately fund finance leases; they are not precise statutory EPS forecasts after adoption. Contract prices, wins/losses, cost responses, project cash timing and minority attribution remain material model uncertainties.

Underlying data retain full precision. The table rounds billions and EPS to two decimals. Bear/base/bull cases and causal sensitivities are in the full download; they are conditional scenarios, not statistical confidence ranges. Future unannounced acquisition earnings/cash, relay fees and subsidies are zero.
