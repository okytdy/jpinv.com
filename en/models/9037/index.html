Assumptions

  • Revenue: existing center and trucking activity is flat. Center wins add 4.5% of prior-year sales, against 1% churn. Center/trucking prices rise 2%/2.2% annually, anchored to customer starts and reported segment trends.
  • Margins: wages rise 3.5% and other variable inputs 1.5%, offset by assumed center/truck productivity gains of 1.4%/1.7%. Pricing, new work and productivity lift OP; these are JII estimates rather than disclosed unit economics.
  • Investment and funding: FY3/28–30 capex is ¥13.30bn/¥11.62bn/¥12.24bn, including named projects. Annual bank repayments are ¥3.5bn; funding depends on preserving a ¥15bn cash reserve.
  • EPS: tax is 33.5%, with separate subsidiary minority claims and 74.050m later-year basic shares. No new acquisition or buyback is assumed. Operating rents remain expensed; the new lease standard’s statutory effect is unquantified.
