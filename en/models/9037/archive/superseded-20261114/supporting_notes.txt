An assumed ¥15bn available-cash reserve triggers model funding; lender availability is not assured. Lease accounting Constant pre-adoption operating-rent basis; finance-lease principal ¥2.1bn/year. New lease-standard effects from April 2027 remain unquantified. The ¥6.702bn opening finance-lease balance is a March 2026 proxy, not a separately disclosed September amount. Tax, minorities and shares Tax 33.5%; Kinbutsu earnings proxy +5%; residual minority-claim pool +4%; 74.050222m later basic shares. Separate parent/minority claims follow tax. FY3/27 weighted shares differ from later years; no future buyback or new dilution is assumed. Distributions and acquisitions Future parent dividends 30% of parent earnings; minority dividends 28%; no unannounced acquisition, subsidy or relay-fee income. Future payout and acquisition assumptions are JII estimates. FCF is before dividends and bank repayments and does not equal an increase in cash. Sources and calculation basis Primary evidence: FY3/26 results ; H1 FY3/27 results,30 October 2026 ; FY3/26 annual securities report ; and FY3/26 supplemental presentation . Exact locators and observed-versus-assumed input classifications are retained in the assumption register. The company plans to adopt the new lease standard from April 2027 and has not quantified its effect. These recurring economic estimates retain operating rents in costs and separately fund finance leases; they are not precise statutory EPS forecasts after adoption. Contract prices, wins/losses, cost responses, project cash timing and minority attribution remain material model uncertainties. Underlying data retain full precision. The table rounds billions and EPS to two decimals. Bear/base/bull cases and causal sensitivities are in the full download; they are conditional scenarios, not statistical confidence ranges. Future unannounced acquisition earnings/cash, relay fees and subsidies are zero.
