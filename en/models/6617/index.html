Sources and calculation basis

Reported figures and commitments come from Takaoka Toko’s H1 FY3/27 financial results (30 October 2026), FY3/26 annual securities report (25 June 2026) and medium-term plan and capital allocation (28 April 2026). The National Tax Agency’s defense-tax guidance informs the tax assessment; 31.5% remains JII’s group-rate assumption. Specific operating and disposal sources are linked beside the assumptions above.

Figures use the frozen 3 November refined independent model. Calculations retain full precision; this page rounds billions to one decimal and EPS to two decimals. Forecasts retain an after-rent economic basis; the quantified April 2027 lease-accounting impact is not disclosed.
