Assumptions

  • Deliveries: Power orders and real-workload capacity grow 4.5% annually after FY3/27. Backlog conversion is constrained by engineering, testing and acceptance. Metering order growth slows to 3%/2%/1% as the replacement cycle matures; these are JII financial proxies.
  • Margins: Power/Metering new-order prices rise 2%/1.5%, versus 1.5% input inflation. Old contracts keep vintage prices. Fixed costs rise about 3%; corporate spending and depreciation increase. Slower metering growth and these costs weigh on later OP.
  • Investment and cash: capex is ¥6.5bn/¥7.5bn/¥7.0bn/¥6.5bn. The estimated ¥8.188bn remaining property receipt in FY3/28 is outside operating FCF; final consideration is undisclosed.
  • EPS: tax is 31.5%; weighted shares fall from 15.583m to 15.400m, then stay flat. No additional buyback is assumed.
