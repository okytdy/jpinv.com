Sources and calculation basis

Primary evidence: Kamakura’s FY1/26 annual securities report (23 April 2026), H1 FY1/27 report (11 September 2026), annual results presentation and H1 results presentation, plus subsidiary accounts linked above. The complete source index retains exact locators and archive hashes.

Revenue uses seven management business categories, with an explicit scope/rounding reconciliation; the company has one statutory segment. Advertising, staffing and other-cost responses are bounded JII financial proxies. Subsidiary fiscal dates and purchase accounting prevent a full reconciliation to reported H1 minority profit; the full model tests a positive annual nonparent claim of ¥60m. Cash is consolidated and not wholly distributable.

The original illustrative 18/20/22x P/E choices, 10% annual discount and ¥20 dividend assumptions are retained to isolate the forecast revision. They are JII choices, not newly measured peer multiples. At the 2 November 2026 close of ¥579, the base fair-value gap is 24.9% (fair value/price−1). The three-year horizon describes the valuation calculation. Option-adjusted EPS includes the full identified overhang and is not GAAP diluted EPS.

Numbers retain full precision in the download. The overview rounds billions to two decimals and EPS to two decimals. Future leases retain a comparable after-rent basis; the issuer’s FY1/29 accounting-transition effect is unquantified.
