Assumptions

  • Growth: seven business lines are forecast separately. FY1/28–30 care revenue growth slows from 30% to 20%, municipal services from 23% to 18%, funerals from 16% to 12%, and graves from 9% to 7%. H1 referrals, sales and municipal partnerships anchor these JII estimates.
  • Margins: advertising follows noninsurance sales; wages rise 3%, with hiring responding to the care/municipal mix. Other costs rise by 2% inflation plus 60% of incremental activity growth. Costs grow more slowly than revenue, lifting margins.
  • Investment: annual cash capex rises from ¥0.60bn to ¥0.75bn; software spending feeds depreciation rather than becoming a free earnings benefit.
  • EPS: future tax is 31.5% before nondeductible goodwill. Separate minority claims and 41.195m basic shares are retained; no new acquisition or buyback is assumed.
