H1 p17 ↗ Scope and limits Delivery capacity, recognition schedules and costs use bounded financial proxies. They do not represent disclosed physical volumes or project-level budgets. Cash is an approximate financing check, not distributable surplus; bonus/other-liability, pension and investment cash timing are not fully isolated. A required stress borrowing is conditional financing, not proof of lending availability. No unexecuted buyback or future acquisition raises base EPS. Acquisition EPS is not separately isolated. Future lease-accounting adoption is unquantified, so the forecast retains a consistent after-rent economic basis. Current H1 actuals are fixed. New estimates replace the earlier published Dai-Dan revenue/margin recipe; they do not renew the other companies’ forecasts.
