Why OP and EPS change each year

Higher delivered revenue adds profit, while staffing/pay and investment raise overhead. EPS also reflects tax, minority and weighted-share conversion: FY3/28’s EPS increase includes ¥8.24/share from the actual-H1 to forward conversion basis.

Scope and limits

Delivery capacity, recognition schedules and costs use bounded financial proxies. They do not represent disclosed physical volumes or project-level budgets. Cash is an approximate financing check, not distributable surplus; bonus/other-liability, pension and investment cash timing are not fully isolated. A required stress borrowing is conditional financing, not proof of lending availability.

No unexecuted buyback or future acquisition raises base EPS. Acquisition EPS is not separately isolated. Future lease-accounting adoption is unquantified, so the forecast retains a consistent after-rent economic basis.

Current H1 actuals are fixed. New estimates replace the earlier published Dai-Dan revenue/margin recipe; they do not renew the other companies’ forecasts.
