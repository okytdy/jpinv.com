Key Assumptions

  • Profit retention: the base carries forward the full observed improvement in like-for-like half-year cash gross margin, consistent with improved bidding and execution. This is the most important judgment: retaining half would reduce FY3/30 EPS from ¥319 to about ¥297.
  • Delivery: staffing grows 5.03% annually, anchored to the latest full-year group headcount increase; productivity adds 1% from FY3/28. These are JII capacity proxies, not disclosed physical output.
  • Pricing and costs: new bids and nonlabor inputs rise 4% annually; pay rises 5%, informed by the disclosed three-year base-pay increase. Existing bids retain their original prices.
  • Margins and EPS: overhead grows faster than sales, easing OP margin from 17.5% to 17.0%. The model uses a 31.01% normalized tax rate, separate minority claims and no unexecuted buybacks.
